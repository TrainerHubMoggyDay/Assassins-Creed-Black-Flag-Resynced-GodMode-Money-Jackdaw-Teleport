- Infinite Oxygen

- Defense Multiplier
- Oxygen Drain Rate

### Jackdaw
- Ship: Infinite Health
- Ship: Infinite Crews
- Ship: Infinite Ammo
- Ship: Instant Weapon Cooldown
- Ship: Super Damage / One Hit Kills
- Ship: Damage Multiplier
- Ship: Defense Multiplier

### Combat

- Easy Break Defense
